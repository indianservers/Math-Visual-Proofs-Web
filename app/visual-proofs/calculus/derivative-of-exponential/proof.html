🤯 THE SURPRISING DISCOVERY
For this special curve, something amazing happens…
HEIGHT = STEEPNESS
The curve's height tells us exactly how fast it is growing!
1📏 How can we measure steepness?

Pick two points on the curve. The line connecting them helps us estimate how steep the curve is between those two spots.

2🔍 What if we bring the points closer?

Drag Q toward P. As the points get closer and closer, the connecting line gives a much better picture of the curve at that exact point.

3🎯 What happens when they almost meet?

The line becomes the TANGENT — a line that touches the curve at just one point! This tells us the steepness right at P.

4🤯 The Mind-Blowing Part

Look at the height and the steepness! They are exactly equal. Height tells us how fast ex is growing!
