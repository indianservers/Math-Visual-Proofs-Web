✨ Why it works
1 The Curve and Fixed Point P

Consider the smooth parabola f(x) = 0.5x². Fix a point P(x₀, y₀) on the curve.

P = (x₀, f(x₀))
2 Secant Line to Second Point Q

Choose a second point Q at distance Δx: Q(x₀ + Δx, f(x₀ + Δx)). Draw the secant line passing through P and Q.

\text{Slope} = \frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x}
3 Shrinking the Interval Δx → 0

Slide point Q along the curve towards P as Δx shrinks. The secant line continuously rotates toward a limiting orientation.

\Delta x \to 0
4 The Tangent Line Limit

When Δx reaches 0, the secant line becomes the tangent line. Under high magnification, the curve is indistinguishable from this tangent line!

f'(x₀) = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x}
Make a prediction
What geometric line does the secant line become in the limit as Δx approaches 0?
Mathematical Invariant
Secant Slope = \frac{f(x + Δx) - f(x)}{Δx}
Tangent Slope = \lim_{Δx \to 0} \frac{f(x + Δx) - f(x)}{Δx}
= f'(x)
✓ Secant line smoothly morphs into tangent.
🧩 Build the one-line proof

Assemble the fundamental algebraic invariants in sequence to complete the formal proof.

PEDAGOGICAL STUDY GUIDE

Derivative as Tangent Secant Zoom

💡 Real-World Intuitive Metaphor

A TV screen with aspect ratio p:h matching h:q: the geometric mean height h satisfies h/p = q/h, so the square on the altitude h² equals the rectangle p × q.

🏛️ Historical Origin & Insight

Euclid’s Elements, Book VI, Proposition 8 (Altitude Theorem).

⚠️ Common Traps & Misconceptions to Avoid

Don’t confuse geometric mean with arithmetic mean! The arithmetic mean is (p + q)/2; the geometric mean is √(pq).

📐 Formal Proof Derivation
1
Parabola function:
f(x) = x²
2
Difference quotient for secant slope:
\frac{f(x + \Delta x) - f(x)}{\Delta x} = \frac{(x + \Delta x)² - x²}{\Delta x}
3
Expand the numerator:
= \frac{x² + 2x\Delta x + (\Delta x)² - x²}{\Delta x} = \frac{2x\Delta x + (\Delta x)²}{\Delta x}
4
Divide through by \Delta x (for \Delta x \neq 0):
= 2x + \Delta x
5
Take the limit as \Delta x \to 0:
f'(x) = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} (2x + \Delta x) = 2x ■
