Learn About the Ellipse

1 What is an ellipse?
▼

An ellipse is the locus of all points in a plane for which the sum of distances from two fixed points (called foci) is constant.

PF₁ + PF₂ = 2a

Key features:

  • P = (x, y) is any point on the ellipse.
  • F₁ = (-c, 0) and F₂ = (c, 0) are the two foci.
  • 2a is the total length of the major axis.
2 Constant-sum property
▼

As point P moves anywhere around the ellipse, individual distance lengths PF₁ and PF₂ change continuously, but their sum remains perfectly equal to 2a.

PF₁ = 5.12
PF₂ = 3.88
Sum = 9.00 (Target 2a = 9.00)

When P moves towards F₁, PF₁ decreases while PF₂ increases by the exact same amount!

3 The two foci
▼

The foci F₁ and F₂ are symmetrically placed interior points along the major axis at distance c from the center:

F₁ = (-c, 0),   F₂ = (c, 0)
Current Foci:
F₁ = (-2.70, 0)
F₂ = (2.70, 0)
4 Semi-major axis a
▼

The semi-major axis a is half the longest diameter of the ellipse. The full major axis extends horizontally from (-a, 0) to (a, 0) with length 2a.

a = 4.50  |  Major Axis (2a) = 9.00
5 Semi-minor axis b
▼

The semi-minor axis b is half the shortest diameter of the ellipse. The minor axis extends vertically from (0, -b) to (0, b) with length 2b.

b = 3.60  |  Minor Axis (2b) = 7.20
6 Distance c
▼

The focal distance c satisfies the Pythagorean relationship for ellipses:

c² = a² - b²  ⇒  c = √(a² - b²)
c = √(4.50² - 3.60²) = √(20.25 - 12.96) = 2.70
7 Eccentricity
▼

The eccentricity e measures how "stretched" or non-circular the ellipse is:

e = c / a (0 < e < 1)
e = 2.70 / 4.50 = 0.60

If e → 0, the ellipse becomes a perfect circle (a = b). As e → 1, the ellipse becomes flatter.

8 Deriving the equation
▼

Starting from distance equality PF₁ + PF₂ = 2a:

  1. Step 1: Distance formula:
    √((x+c)² + y²) + √((x-c)² + y²) = 2a
  2. Step 2: Isolate one radical & square:
    (x+c)² + y² = (2a - √((x-c)²+y²))²
  3. Step 3: Simplify to isolate remaining radical:
    a√((x-c)²+y²) = a² - cx
  4. Step 4: Square again & substitute b² = a² - c²:
    (a²-c²)x² + a²y² = a²(a²-c²) ⇒ b²x² + a²y² = a²b²
  5. Step 5: Divide by a²b²:
x²/a² + y²/b² = 1
9 Why sum is constant
▼

Consider the vertex point at (a, 0):

  • Distance to F₁: a + c
  • Distance to F₂: a - c
  • Sum: (a + c) + (a - c) = 2a

Because the curve is defined by this invariant length, the sum PF₁ + PF₂ is strictly conserved across all points.

▼

x = ± a / e = ± a² / c
