Learn About the Hyperbola

1 What is a hyperbola?
▼

A hyperbola is the locus of all points in a plane for which the absolute difference between the distances to two fixed points (foci) is constant.

|PF₁ - PF₂| = 2a

Where:

  • P = (x, y) is any point on the two open branches.
  • F₁ = (-c, 0) and F₂ = (c, 0) are the two foci.
  • 2a is the fixed distance length of the transverse axis.
2 The two foci
▼

The foci F₁ and F₂ lie symmetrically along the transverse axis at distance c from the center:

c = √(a² + b²)  ⇒  c > a
Current Foci:
F₁ = (-3.606, 0)
F₂ = (3.606, 0)
Focal Distance F₁F₂ = 2c = 7.212
3 Constant difference
▼

As point P slides along either branch, distance lengths PF₁ and PF₂ vary continuously, but their absolute difference stays exactly equal to 2a:

PF₁ = 8.244
PF₂ = 4.244
|PF₁ - PF₂| = 4.000 (Target 2a = 4.000)
4 Vertices
▼

The vertices V₁ and V₂ are the turning points of the two hyperbola branches on the focal axis:

V₁ = (-a, 0),   V₂ = (a, 0)
V₁ = (-2.00, 0)  |  V₂ = (2.00, 0)
Distance between vertices = 2a = 4.00
5 Center
▼

The center O(0,0) is the midpoint between both vertices V₁, V₂ and both foci F₁, F₂. It serves as the point of rotational symmetry for both branches.

6 Asymptotes
▼

The asymptotes are boundary lines that the hyperbola branches approach infinitely closely as |x| → ∞, without ever intersecting them:

y = ± (b / a) x
y = ± (3.00 / 2.00) x = ± 1.50x
7 Eccentricity
▼

The eccentricity e quantifies the flare or opening angle of the hyperbola branches:

e = c / a (e > 1 always)
e = 3.606 / 2.00 = 1.803 > 1
Conic Section Comparison:
• Circle: e = 0
• Ellipse: 0 < e < 1
• Parabola: e = 1
• Hyperbola: e > 1
8 Directrices
▼

The directrices of a horizontal hyperbola are vertical lines positioned between the center and the vertices at distance a/e = a²/c:

x = ± a / e = ± a² / c
x = ± 2.00² / 3.606 = ± 1.109
9 Deriving the equation
▼

Derivation from distance difference |PF₁ - PF₂| = 2a:

  1. Step 1: Distance formula:
    √((x+c)²+y²) - √((x-c)²+y²) = ±2a
  2. Step 2: Isolate radical & square:
    (x+c)² + y² = (2a + √((x-c)²+y²))²
  3. Step 3: Simplify to isolate remaining radical:
    cx - a² = a√((x-c)²+y²)
  4. Step 4: Square again & substitute c² = a² + b²:
    (c²-a²)x² - a²y² = a²(c²-a²) ⇒ b²x² - a²y² = a²b²
  5. Step 5: Divide by a²b²:
x²/a² - y²/b² = 1
10 Why locus forms hyperbola
▼

Consider vertex V₂ = (a, 0):

  • Distance to F₁: c + a
  • Distance to F₂: c - a
  • Difference: (c + a) - (c - a) = 2a

Because points nearer F₂ make PF₂ small and PF₁ large (and vice versa for points near F₁), two symmetrical unbounded branches form.
