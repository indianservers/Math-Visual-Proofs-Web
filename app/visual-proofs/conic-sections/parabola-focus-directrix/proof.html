Learn About the Parabola

1 What is a parabola?
▼

A parabola is the locus of all points that are equally distant from a fixed point called the focus and a fixed line called the directrix.

PF = PH

Where:

  • P = (x, y) is any point on the curve.
  • F = (0, p) is the fixed focus point.
  • H = (x, -p) is the perpendicular foot of P on the directrix.
  • PF is the distance from point P to Focus F.
  • PH is the perpendicular distance from point P to Directrix H.

3 Directrix
▼

The directrix is a fixed horizontal line located outside the parabola, perpendicular to the axis of symmetry and parallel to the x-axis.

y = -p

The vertex V(0,0) lies exactly halfway between the focus and the directrix at distance p from each.

7 Deriving the equation
▼

Follow the algebraic derivation from distance equality:

  1. Step 1: Distance Equality
    PF = PH
  2. Step 2: Apply Distance Formula
    √(x² + (y-p)²) = |y + p|
  3. Step 3: Square both sides
    x² + (y - p)² = (y + p)²
  4. Step 4: Expand binomials
    x² + y² - 2py + p² = y² + 2py + p²
  5. Step 5: Simplify terms
    x² = 4py
x² = 6.00y
8 How to construct a parabola
▼

Step-by-step geometric construction:

  1. Choose focal distance p > 0.
  2. Place focus at F(0, p).
  3. Draw directrix horizontal line y = -p.
  4. Choose a candidate point P(x, y).
  5. Drop perpendicular from P to directrix at H(x, -p).
  6. Measure lengths PF and PH.
  7. Point belongs to parabola if PF = PH.
  8. Repeat for many points to trace the parabola.
