✧Why it works

1Form a right triangle.
2Use Pythagorean Theorem.

CP² = (Δx)² + (Δy)²

3Every point P satisfies CP = r.

So all points form a circle.

CP² = r²

What equation describes this circle?
Use the two coordinate differences to build CP².

One-line proof

CP² = (x − h)² + (y − k)²⇒CP = r⇒
