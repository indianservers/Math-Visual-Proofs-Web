✧   Why it works

1Drop perpendiculars from B to form a right triangle with A.
2The horizontal leg has length Δx.
The vertical leg has length Δy.
3By the Pythagorean Theorem, the hypotenuse has length d.
d = √((x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²)
