Point-Slope Form of a Line ☆

Mission: Drag point P anywhere on the line. The rise/run from fixed point A stays the same.
Intermediate
◷   8 min
● Fixed point A A ( 2 , 1 )
Slope (m)3/2  = 1.5
● Moving point P P ( 8 , 10 )
Rise / Run Δy/Δx  = 9/6  = 3/2

✧ Why it works

1Draw rise and run from A to P.
2The slope Δy/Δx is constant for all points on the line.
3Rewrite rise/run using coordinates to get the point-slope form.
Δy = mΔx  ⟶  y − y₁ = m(x − x₁)
y − y₁ = m(x − x₁)
○ Move P and identify the invariant.
