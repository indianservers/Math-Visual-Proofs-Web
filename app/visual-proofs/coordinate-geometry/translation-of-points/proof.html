✧   Why it works

1Every point moves by the same vector (a, b).
2Only the position changes.

Shape and orientation stay the same.

Side lengths and angles are preserved.

Translation proof

For every original point P(x, y), add the same vector (a, b). Its image is P′(x + a, y + b).

Because the same displacement is applied to every vertex, differences between vertices do not change. Therefore corresponding side lengths, angles, shape, and orientation are preserved.

The grid picture is an illustration; the coordinate rule works for every point in the plane.

One-line proof

P(x, y)+(a, b)=P'(x + a, y + b)

Adding the same vector to every point shifts the figure without changing lengths or angles.
