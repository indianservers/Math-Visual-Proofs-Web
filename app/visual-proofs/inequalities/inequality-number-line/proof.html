Why it works

1 Direction = Smaller or Larger

A number line is ordered from smaller numbers on the left to larger numbers on the right.

• Moving left means values decrease (< or ≤).
• Moving right means values increase (> or ≥).
2 Marker = Include or Exclude

The circle at the boundary tells us whether the boundary value belongs to the solution set.

Open (excluded)
Closed (included)
Strict (<, >) ⇒ Open | Inclusive (≤, ≥) ⇒ Closed
3 Together = Inequality

Direction + marker describe exactly which numbers satisfy the inequality.
