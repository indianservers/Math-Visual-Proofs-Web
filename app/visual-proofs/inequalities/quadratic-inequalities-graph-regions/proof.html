Why it works

1 The x-intercepts split the line

For f(x) = x² − 2x, the curve crosses the x-axis at x = 0 and x = 2.

2 Parabola direction (a > 0)

So the graph is above the x-axis outside the roots and below between them.

3 Match inequality to regions

For f(x) > 0, take x < 0 or x > 2.
