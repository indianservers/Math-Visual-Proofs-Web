Rule reminder

Dividing or multiplying by a negative number reverses the inequality sign.

× (-1) ↔
◈

Why it works

1 Additive inverse property

To isolate the variable term, undo the constant using its additive inverse on both sides.

2x + 3 − 3 < 11 − 3 ⇒ 2x < 8
2 Multiplicative inverse property

After removing the constant, divide both sides by the coefficient of x.

2x / 2 < 8 / 2 ⇒ x < 4
3 Sign flip rule

Multiplying or dividing an inequality by a negative number reverses the order.

a < b  →  ÷(-c)  →  a/c > b/c
