Why it works
1
Divide a by b.
a = q × b + r
multiple of b + remainder (0 ≤ r < b)
2
Any common divisor of a and b also divides r.
d | a d | b d | r
3
Therefore gcd(a, b) = gcd(b, r).
(a, b) → (b, r) → (r, b mod r)
