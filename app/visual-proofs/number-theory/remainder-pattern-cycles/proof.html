    Repeat by pigeonhole

    Why pigeonhole forces periodicity:
    Because there are only m distinct remainders {0, 1, ..., m−1}, when taking m+1 consecutive numbers, at least two must yield the same remainder by the Pigeonhole Principle. Since each step is deterministic (add 1 mod m), the exact sequence of remainders repeats endlessly with a minimal period of m.
