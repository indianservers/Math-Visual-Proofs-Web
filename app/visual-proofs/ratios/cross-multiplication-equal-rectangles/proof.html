Why It Works (Geometric Proof)
Step 1 — Construct Rectangles Form two rectangles with sides (a, d) and (b, c). Draw both diagonals. a = 3, d = 8 | b = 4, c = 6
Step 2 — Compare Areas The area of the first rectangle is a × d. The area of the second rectangle is b × c. ad = 24 square units | bc = 24 square units
Step 3 — Equivalence Conclusion If the ratios are equivalent (a/b = c/d), both rectangles have EXACTLY EQUAL AREA. 24 = 24 ⇒ Ratios Are Equivalent!
