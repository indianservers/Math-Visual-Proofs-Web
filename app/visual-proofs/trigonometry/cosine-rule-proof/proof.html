Projection idea:　a² = (b − c cos θ)² + (c sin θ)²

✧　Why it works

1Resolve side c into components.
2Use coordinates to form a right triangle.
a² = (b − c cos θ)² + (c sin θ)²
3Apply Pythagoras.
cos²θ + sin²θ = 1
What should a equal when θ = 90°?　⌄
a² = b² + c² − 2bc cos θ
