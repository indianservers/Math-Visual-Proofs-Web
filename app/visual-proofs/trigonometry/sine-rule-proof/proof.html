Sine Rule / Law of Sines　☆

Sides of a triangle are chords of its circumcircle, and each chord has length 2R times the sine of the opposite angle.

Chord-Angle Theorem
In ΔAOB
sin A = BC / 2R = a/2R

In ΔBOC
sin B = AC / 2R = b/2R

In ΔCOA
sin C = AB / 2R = c/2R

✧　Why it works

1Sides are chords
2Sine in a central triangle
3Equal ratios emerge
a/sin A　=　b/sin B　=　c/sin C
What is the common value?　⌄
a/sin A　=　b/sin B　=　c/sin C　=　2R
