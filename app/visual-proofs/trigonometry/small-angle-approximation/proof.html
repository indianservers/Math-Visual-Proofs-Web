Small Angle Approximation: sin θ is approximately θ

Meaning: On the unit circle, sin θ < θ < tan θ for small positive θ, and the three lengths nearly coincide.

✧   Why it works

1Compare on the unit circleFor 0 < θ < π/2, sin θ < θ < tan θ.
2Unwrap the arcArc length θ becomes a line segment — only in radians.
3Lengths nearly coincideFor small θ, all three lengths are almost equal.
sin θ ≈ θ
