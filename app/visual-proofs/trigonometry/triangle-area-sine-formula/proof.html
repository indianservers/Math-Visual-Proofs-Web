Area of a Triangle Using Sine: A = ½ ab sin C

Meaning: A triangle with sides a and b around angle C has height b sin C, so area equals one half ab sin C.

✧   Why it works

1Drop height BD ⟂ CA.
Right triangle CBD has hypotenuse b and angle C.
2In right triangle CBD,
sin C = BD / b   so   BD = b sin C.
3Area of triangle = ½ (base)(height)
= ½ a · (b sin C)
= ½ ab sin C.
