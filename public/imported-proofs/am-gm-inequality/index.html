Why it works 💡

1 Arithmetic Mean (AM)

For two positive numbers a and b, their arithmetic mean is their ordinary average: add the two numbers and divide their sum by 2.

\text{AM} = \frac{a + b}{2}
For a = 5.0, b = 3.0: AM = (5.0 + 3.0) / 2 = 4.000
2 Geometric Mean (GM)

For two positive numbers a and b, their geometric mean is obtained by multiplying the two numbers and taking the square root of their product.

\text{GM} = \sqrt{ab}
For a = 5.0, b = 3.0: GM = \sqrt{5.0 \cdot 3.0} = \sqrt{15.0} \approx 3.873
AM = 4.000
GM = 3.873
Therefore AM > GM
3 The AM-GM Inequality

The arithmetic mean of two positive numbers is always greater than or equal to their geometric mean:

\frac{a + b}{2} \ge \sqrt{ab}
4 How the semicircle represents AM and GM
5 When does equality occur?

Equality occurs exactly when a = b. If a = b = 4:

\text{AM} = \frac{4+4}{2} = 4, \quad \text{GM} = \sqrt{4 \cdot 4} = 4 \implies \text{AM} = \text{GM}
Currently: a = 5.0, b = 3.0 ⇒ AM = 4.000 > GM = 3.873
