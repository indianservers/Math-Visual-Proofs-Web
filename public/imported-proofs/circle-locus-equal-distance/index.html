Why it works
  1. 1
    Locus definition

    A circle is the set of all points P such that the distance from P to the center C is constant radius r.

  2. 2
    Equal distance

    As P moves along the circle, CP stays equal to r. All such points form the circle.

  3. 3
    Equation obtained

    Using distance formula: √((x-h)² + (y-k)²) = r ⇒ (x-h)² + (y-k)² = r²
