✨ Why it works
1
Split
Choose any number > 1 and split it into two smaller factors.
n ➔ a × b
2
Repeat
Keep splitting non-prime factors until all leaves are prime.
n ➔ ... ➔ (primes)
3
Unique (up to order)
The prime leaves are always the same, no matter how you split.
(2)(2)(3)(5) = (2)(2)(3)(5)
What must be true? ▼
One-line visual proof
60
➔ 🌿 ➔ (2)(2)(3)(5) = 2² × 3¹ × 5¹
Every split preserves the product. Reaching primes fixes the multiset of prime factors.
