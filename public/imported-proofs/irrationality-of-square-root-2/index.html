    Why √2 is Irrational

    Mission: Pair the unit-square diagonal with a parity contradiction.

    1 Geometry: The Unit Square

    The diagonal of a unit square has length √2.

    Area = 1
    Diagonal² = 1² + 1² = 2
    Diagonal = √2

    Why it works
    1. Unit square diagonal
    By Pythagorean theorem, diagonal of 1x1 square is √2.
    2. Assume √2 = p/q
    Assume p/q is in lowest terms with no common factor > 1.
    3. Parity contradiction
    2q² = p² forces both p & q to be even, violating lowest terms!
    Prediction
    What happens next?
    If q is even, then p must be even.
    Reveal

    Since p, q can't both be even in lowest terms, the assumption is impossible.

    Therefore, √2 is irrational.
